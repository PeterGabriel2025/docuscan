Use Gemini to transcribe your exam

  1. Tap Open Gemini and keep this DocuScan tab open.
  2. In Gemini, tap the + or attachment button. Take photos or choose them from your phone's gallery. Attach up to 10 images in one message.
  3. Copy the prompt below, paste it into Gemini, and send it with your images.
  4. Copy Gemini's transcription, return to this tab, and paste it into the response box.

Have more than 10 pages? Send them in batches of up to 10. Ask Gemini to continue the transcription in page order, then combine the responses before pasting them here. For a pencil circle around text, Gemini should write it as [CIRCLED:A]; the downloaded PDF will draw a circle around A.
